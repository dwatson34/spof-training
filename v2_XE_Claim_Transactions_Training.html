Concept 1

Claims Financial Lifecycle

How Xuber tracks every pound flowing through a claim from first notification to closure

Every claim in Xuber is a financial ledger. When a claim opens, Xuber tracks what the insurer expects to pay, what has been paid, and what it expects to recover.

The four transaction types

Reserve

Expected outgoing cost

The adjuster's best estimate of total claim cost. Drives financial reporting and solvency calculations. Not money paid out - money set aside.

Payment

Actual money out

Disbursements to claimants, repairers or third parties. Each payment reduces the outstanding reserve.

Recovery Reserve

Expected money back

Set when subrogation or salvage is anticipated. Reduces the net claim cost in reporting.

Recovery Receipt

Actual money in

Money received from third parties or salvage sales. Offsets the recovery reserve and improves net claim cost.

Claim financial lifecycle

FNOL - Claim opened, initial reserve set
Investigation - reserve adjusted as facts emerge
Payment authorised and disbursed
Subrogation identified - recovery reserve set
Recovery received - recovery receipt posted
Claim closed - reserve to zero, net cost calculated
Net claim cost = Payments made minus Recovery receipts. This is what the insurer actually loses on a claim.

Concept 2

Claim Reserve

Setting and adjusting the estimated total cost of a claim

A reserve is an accounting provision. The insurer's best estimate of what the claim will ultimately cost. It must be set at FNOL and kept current throughout the claim lifecycle.

Reserve types in Xuber

Indemnity

Main loss reserve

The core claim cost owed to the claimant. Largest reserve category.

Expenses

Handling costs

Legal fees, surveyor costs, adjusting fees. Tracked separately for management reporting.

TP Indemnity

Third party costs

Liability claims - costs owed to third parties. Can be open-ended.

Ex Gratia

Goodwill payments

Payments without legal obligation. Requires senior authorisation.

Zero reserve = closed: All reserve categories must be zero before a claim can be closed. You cannot close a claim with any outstanding reserve.

Reserve API endpoints

claim-reserve.model.tsTypeScript
// GET /api/v2/claims/:id/reserves
// POST /api/v2/claims/:id/reserves

export interface ClaimReserve {
  id:           string;
  claimId:      string;
  reserveType:
    'Indemnity' |
    'Expenses'  |
    'TPIndemnity' |
    'ExGratia';
  amount:       number;
  currency:     string;
  reason:       string;
  approvalStatus:
    'Pending' |
    'Approved' |
    'Rejected';
}
Reserves are permanent records. You cannot delete or edit a reserve transaction. To reduce a reserve, post a new reserve for the lower amount. Every movement is in the audit trail.

Concept 3

Claim Payment

Processing payments to claimants, solicitors and third parties

A claim payment is money going out. Every payment in Xuber requires an authorised reserve, a valid payee, and supervisor approval above threshold before it can be processed.

Payment workflow

1

Payment request raised

Handler creates a request. System checks their authority limit. If over limit, routes to supervisor for approval.

2

Payee verification

Xuber checks payee bank details are on file and verified. Unverified payees block payment processing.

3

Authorisation

Authoriser reviews and approves. System logs approver name, timestamp, and authority level used.

4

Disbursement

Payment batched and sent to the banking system. When confirmed, Xuber marks it paid and reduces the outstanding reserve.

claim-payment.model.tsTypeScript
export interface ClaimPayment {
  id:          string;
  claimId:     string;
  reserveId:   string;
  paymentType:
    'Indemnity' |
    'Expenses'  |
    'ExGratia';
  amount:      number;
  currency:    string;
  payeeId:     string;
  payeeType:
    'Claimant'   |
    'Solicitor'  |
    'ThirdParty' |
    'Repairer';
  status:
    'Draft'      |
    'Authorised' |
    'Sent'       |
    'Cleared';
}
Payment cannot exceed reserve. Xuber validates that the payment amount does not exceed the outstanding reserve for the same type. A 400 error is returned if it does.

Concept 4

Recovery Reserve

Estimating expected recoveries - subrogation, salvage and contribution

When an insurer expects to recover money from a third party - a negligent driver's insurer, or salvage from a written-off vehicle - a Recovery Reserve estimates that incoming amount and reduces the reported net claim cost.

recovery-reserve.model.tsTypeScript
export interface RecoveryReserve {
  id:              string;
  claimId:         string;
  recoveryType:
    'Subrogation'  |
    'Salvage'      |
    'Contribution' |
    'Reinsurance';
  estimatedAmount: number;
  currency:        string;
  thirdPartyId?:   string;
  status:
    'Open'               |
    'PartiallyRecovered' |
    'FullyRecovered'     |
    'Abandoned';
}

Recovery types

Subrogation

Third-party negligence

Insurer pursues the negligent party or their insurer to recover claim costs paid to the insured.

Salvage

Asset sale proceeds

Money from selling a total-loss vehicle or written-off property.

Contribution

Co-insurer share

Where multiple insurers cover the same risk, contribution is the recovery from the other insurer's share.

Reinsurance

Treaty recovery

Recovery from the reinsurer under the treaty for losses above the retention.

Impact on net cost: A 100,000 claim with a 60,000 recovery reserve shows net cost of 40,000 in management reports.

Concept 5

Recovery Receipt

Recording actual money received and matching it to the recovery reserve

A Recovery Receipt records money actually received. It matches against the Recovery Reserve and immediately reduces the net claim cost in real-time reporting.

Receipt processing rules

Matching

Must reference a reserve

A receipt must link to an existing Open or Partially Recovered recovery reserve.

Partial receipts

Multiple receipts allowed

If recovery arrives in instalments, multiple receipts can be posted. Status becomes Partially Recovered until the full amount is received.

Over-recovery

Receipt exceeds reserve

Xuber allows this. Status becomes Fully Recovered and the surplus is noted in the audit trail.

Real-time

Immediate net cost update

Posting a receipt immediately reduces the net incurred cost. Management reports update in real time.
