# Xuber SPOF Training Suite — User Guide

Welcome! This guide will help you get the most out of the SPOF Training Suite.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Go to the portal:** https://dwatson34.github.io/spof-training/
2. **Click a tab** — Choose from Jump In, What You'll Get, Teams, or Documentation
3. **Click "Browse All Modules"** to see all 24 training modules
4. **Pick a module** and click "Launch" to start learning
5. **Complete the quiz** (70% to pass) to track your progress

---

## What Are SPOFs?

A **Single Point of Failure (SPOF)** is any part of a system that, if it fails, brings down the entire system. This training helps you identify, understand, and eliminate SPOFs in Xuber Enterprise and Genius systems.

---

## How the Training Works

### Each Module Contains

- **7-8 interactive slides** with real code examples
- **Text-to-speech narration** — listen while you learn
- **10 multiple-choice questions** at the end
- **70% pass threshold** — you need 7 out of 10 correct
- **Progress tracking** — your scores are saved in your browser

### Time Commitment

Each module takes about **45–75 minutes** depending on your pace. You can pause and come back anytime.

### Learning Levels

- **Beginner** — Foundation concepts and basics
- **Intermediate** — Hands-on patterns and real-world scenarios
- **Expert** — Advanced techniques and best practices

## FAQ

**Q: Can I retake a module?**
A: Yes! You can retake any module as many times as you want. Your latest score is recorded.

**Q: Do I have to take modules in order?**
A: No, but we recommend following the learning paths above for best results.

**Q: Where are my quiz scores saved?**
A: They're saved in your browser's local storage. Clear your browser data and they'll be lost, so take screenshots if you need them!

**Q: Can I download the modules?**
A: All modules are self-contained HTML files and work completely offline once loaded.

**Q: What if I find a bug or have feedback?**
A: Check the GitHub repository: https://github.com/dwatson34/spof-training

**Q: How do I access the full documentation?**
A: Click the **Documentation** tab on the main page for links to detailed guides.

## Tips for Success

1. **Take notes** — Write down key concepts as you learn
2. **Pause and think** — Don't rush through the slides
3. **Re-read code examples** — Understanding real code is crucial
4. **Retake failed quizzes** — A 70% pass isn't mastery; aim for 90%+
5. **Follow the learning paths** — They're designed to build on each other
